What is the cost of interior design in Delhi NCR?▼
1BHK starts from ₹2.5L, 2BHK from ₹4L, and 3BHK from ₹6L for full-home interiors. Keyvendors modular solutions start at ₹99/sq ft. We provide a detailed, itemised free quote after a site visit — no hidden charges.
How long does a home interior project take?▼
A standard 2BHK takes 45–60 days from design approval to handover. This includes 3D design (7–10 days), material sourcing (7–14 days), and on-site execution (30–40 days). We provide a written timeline commitment.
Which areas in Delhi NCR do you cover?▼
We cover all of Delhi NCR — Ghaziabad, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad, and Delhi. Our strongest presence is in Indirapuram, Vaishali, Crossing Republik, Sector 62/137 Noida, Dwarka, and DLF phases in Gurugram.
Do you show 3D designs before work begins?▼
Every project starts with photorealistic 3D renders — furniture placement, colours, materials, and lighting — all approved before work begins. Design-stage changes are completely free.
What warranty do you offer?▼
10-year structural warranty on civil work and 5-year warranty on all materials and finishes. After handover, our support team handles service calls — no extra charge for the first year.
Is EMI available for interior design projects?▼
Yes. No-cost EMI is available on projects above ₹3 lakh. We also offer a transparent 4-milestone payment plan: 10% booking, 40% design approval, 40% work commencement, 10% on final handover.
How do I get started?▼
Book a free site visit online or via WhatsApp. We visit your property within 24 hours, assess your space, and provide a detailed quote within 48 hours — completely free, no commitment.
