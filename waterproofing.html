What is the cost of waterproofing in Delhi NCR?▼
Terrace waterproofing starts at ₹28/sq ft, basement waterproofing from ₹45/sq ft, and bathroom waterproofing from ₹8,000 per bathroom. Rates include Fosroc/Dr. Fixit materials, labour, and warranty. We provide a free, itemised quote after site inspection — no hidden charges.
Which waterproofing materials do you use?▼
We use only certified, branded materials: Fosroc Brushbond, Dr. Fixit Bathseal, SikaTop Seal 107, Fosroc Nitoseal for expansion joints, and polyurethane injection grouts for basements. No local or unbranded substitutes — ever.
How long does waterproofing take?▼
A standard terrace (1,000–2,000 sq ft) takes 4–6 days. Bathroom waterproofing takes 1–2 days per bathroom. Basement projects take 5–10 days depending on area and severity. We provide a written timeline before work begins.
What warranty do you provide on waterproofing?▼
10-year written warranty on terrace, basement, and injection grouting work. 7-year warranty on bathroom waterproofing. 5-year warranty on external wall treatment. If it leaks within the warranty period, we fix it free of charge.
Can you fix bathroom leaks without removing tiles?▼
Yes. We use Dr. Fixit's non-invasive injection and sealant systems to fix bathroom seepage without tile removal in most cases. This saves time, cost, and the hassle of retiling. Our thermal imaging confirms the exact leak source first.
Which areas in Delhi NCR do you serve?▼
We cover all of Delhi NCR — Ghaziabad, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad, and all Delhi zones. Our strongest presence is in Indirapuram, Vaishali, Crossing Republik, Sector 62/137 Noida, Dwarka, Rohini, and DLF phases in Gurugram.
